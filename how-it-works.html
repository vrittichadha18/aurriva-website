How it works

A real person on the other side of the screen.

Aurriva guides you through the first days somewhere new, by video call, with a certified local Navigator who knows the place.

The problem

Culture is learned from a person, not a page.

Moving somewhere new is hard in ways nobody warns you about. Google can give you an address. It cannot tell you how to ask a professor for help, or who to call when the first week gets lonely.

The logistics

Banking, SIM cards, transit and scams to avoid, all at once, in the first days.

The unwritten rules

How much to tip, how to talk to a professor or a manager, and how to ask for help.

The first week alone

You are far from home with no local network. The loneliness tends to hit hardest in the first week.

1M+

More than 1 million international students study in the US each year. Their families pay tens of thousands in tuition with no one on the ground to help. Relocating professionals face the same first week, often with a family and a new job to start at the same time.

Source: IIE Open Doors

Three steps

From first call to first week.

  1. Book a free Aura Check

    A 15-minute video call. Tell us where you are headed and what worries you most.
